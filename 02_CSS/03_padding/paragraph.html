The ocean is home to some of the most fascinating and diverse ecosystems on our planet, yet much of it remains completely unexplored. Hidden deep beneath the surface lie vast coral reefs, mysterious underwater trenches, and unique sea creatures that look like they belong in a science fiction movie. Marine biologists estimate that we have discovered only a small fraction of the life that exists in the deep sea. Protecting these waters is crucial, as the health of our oceans directly impacts the stability of global weather patterns and the air we breathe every day.
